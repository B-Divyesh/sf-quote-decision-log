Quote Decision policy

Privacy

Effective 30 August 2026

Local by default

Quote Decision stores quotes, reviews, client decision receipts, and cached license status in your browser on your device. Client receipts use a separate local database, so they remain available on the original client link. We do not operate an account or quote-sync service. The app has no analytics or advertising trackers.

What leaves your device

When you purchase or verify a license, the app contacts the Sociobot billing API. Sociobot and Dodo process the purchase and license token under their policies. The app sends the license token for verification. It does not send your quotes.

A client link contains the selected quote version in its URL fragment. Browsers do not send URL fragments to web servers. Anyone you share that link with can read its contents. A decision receipt is created on the client’s device and moves only when that person shares the file.

Your controls

Use Data and license to export JSON or CSV, import a backup, or delete all local quotes. A client can download or delete a saved local receipt from the original link. Browser site-data controls can remove both databases and the license cache. Uninstalling the PWA may not remove browser data automatically.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through sociobot.in.
