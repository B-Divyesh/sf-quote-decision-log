Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026

Local by default

Quote Decision stores quotes, reviews, client decisions, and cached license status in your browser on your device. We do not operate an account or quote-sync service, and the app contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

What leaves your device

When you purchase or verify a license, the app contacts the Sociobot billing API. Sociobot and its merchant-of-record provider Dodo process the purchase and license token under their policies. The app sends the license token for verification; it does not send your quotes.

A client link contains the selected quote version in the URL fragment. Browsers do not send URL fragments to web servers, but anyone you share that link with can read its contents. A decision receipt is a file created on the client’s device and moves only when that person shares it.

Your controls

Use Data and license to export JSON or CSV, import a backup, or delete all local quotes. Browser site-data controls can also remove the database and license cache. Uninstalling the PWA may not remove browser data automatically.

Contact
